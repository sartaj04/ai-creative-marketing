# Pixo — AI Creative Marketing Platform

## What Pixo Is

Pixo is an AI-powered creative marketing platform that helps businesses **create, distribute, and learn from ads and social content** — without needing designers, agencies, or multiple tools.

Pixo sits at the intersection of:
- **High-quality creative generation** (visual + copy)
- **Platform-aware distribution** (social + ads)
- **Engagement intelligence** (what works, where, and why)

The goal is simple:
> Generate ads and social posts that don’t look AI-made — and get smarter with every campaign.

---

## Core Problem We Solve

Today’s marketing stack is broken:
- Canva is visual but manual
- AI copy tools are text-only
- Ad platforms are complex and fragmented
- Analytics tools don’t connect back to creatives

Marketers are forced to:
- Jump between tools
- Guess what works
- Recreate similar creatives repeatedly
- Rely on agencies for scale

**Pixo replaces this fragmented workflow with a single creative intelligence system.**

## What Pixo Does (High-Level)

### 1. Brand Understanding
- Scrapes a website URL
- Extracts:
  - Brand name & description
  - Colors & typography hints
  - Logos & images
- Builds a persistent brand profile

### 2. Template-Based Creative Generation
- Uses curated, high-quality templates
- AI selects the right template based on:
  - Industry
  - Platform
  - Objective
- AI fills templates with:
  - Headlines
  - Copy
  - CTAs
  - Hashtags
- Outputs platform-ready creatives (image/video + text)

### 3. Multi-Platform Distribution
- Social platforms:
  - Instagram
  - LinkedIn
  - Twitter/X
  - Facebook
- Ad platforms:
  - Meta Ads
  - Google Ads (later phase)

### 4. Engagement & Performance Tracking
- Tracks:
  - Likes
  - Comments
  - Shares
  - Impressions
  - CTR (where available)
- Builds dashboards per:
  - Brand
  - Campaign
  - Template
  - Platform

### 5. Feedback Loop (Key Differentiator)
- Learns which:
  - Templates work best
  - Copy styles perform better
  - Platforms convert for each brand
- Feeds insights back into future generations
